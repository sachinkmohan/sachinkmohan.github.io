## ADRs

Architecture Decision Records capture _what_ was decided and _why_, so future readers (and agents) don't undo deliberate choices.

- [Video: How to use ADRs](https://youtu.be/vRoqBBI51Vo) – A walkthrough of what ADRs are and how to use them.
- [ADR template (Kent C. Dodds)](https://kcd-slides-bwk-adrs-for-agents.pages.dev/samples/adr-template.md) – A ready-to-copy template for writing your own ADRs.
