# Context

Glossary for the site's content types.

## Terms

**Wiki**
The site section that holds all Topics. Living, not dated.
_Avoid_: resources page, bookmarks

**Resource**
A recommended thing (article, tool, book, course, video, person) with a required Note and an optional URL. Belongs to exactly one Topic.
_Avoid_: link, bookmark

**Note**
The author's short explanation (one line is enough) of why a Resource is worth visiting. Required on every Resource.

**Topic**
A named group of Resources (e.g. Writing, AI) in the Wiki. Each Topic has its own page, which may include introductory prose and sub-headings around its Resources.
_Avoid_: category (that term belongs to TIL)

**Blog post**
A dated piece of writing. A list of Resources is not a Blog post; such lists belong under a Topic.

**TIL**
A short, dated note about something learned, grouped by category.
